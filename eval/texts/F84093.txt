Equally, you can see if you live within our practice boundary below before visiting the NHS website: Catchment Area Click on the link below for a list of the roads in our Practice area: Roads in Our Area Outside of Area Registration Registering with our Practice if You Live Outside Our Catchment Area Tollgate Medical Centre may accept registrations from patients who live outside our normal practice boundary but within Newham. This is known as Out of Area Registration . Patients registered under this arrangement can access routine GP services at the practice in the same way as other registered patients. However, there are some important limitations: We may not be able to provide home visits for patients living outside our practice area. If you require a home visit, you may be advised to contact NHS 111 or an alternative local service. Registration requests are considered on an individual basis and are subject to clinical and practical assessment. We reserve the right to decline or discontinue an out of area registration if it is not safe or practical to provide care. Before registering, we recommend that you consider how you will travel to the practice for appointments and whether our location remains convenient for your healthcare needs. If you would like to apply for an Out of Area Registration, please complete the normal registration forms and the out of area registration agreement that can be found below and return them to information.tollgate@nhs.net Please contact our reception team if you are unsure if we accept your postcode, the map above will only show postcodes we accept within our boundary. Out of Area Registration Practice Agreement Temporary Patient Registrations If you are ill while away from home or if you are not registered with a doctor but need to see one you can receive emergency treatment from the local GP practice for 14 days. After 14 days you will need to register as a temporary or permanent patient. You can be registered as a temporary patient for up to three months. This will allow you to be on the local practice list and still remain a patient of your permanent GP. After three months you will have to re-register as a temporary patient or permanently register with that practice. To register as a temporary patient simply contact the local practice you wish to use. Practices do not have to accept you as a temporary patient although they do have an obligation to offer emergency treatment. You cannot register as a temporary patient at a practice in the town or area where you are already registered. Non-English Speakers These fact sheets have been written to explain the role of UK health services, the National Health Service (NHS), to newly-arrived individuals seeking asylum. They cover issues such as the role of GPs, their function as gatekeepers to the health services, how to register and how to access emergency services. Special care has been taken to ensure that information is given in clear language, and the content and style has been tested with user groups.
