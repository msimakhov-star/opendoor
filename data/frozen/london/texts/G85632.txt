See this leaflet for key information that you will need to know about how to use our services. You will need to register with us before you can have an appointment or order a prescription. Please complete our registration form and we will text you to confirm when you are registered – this could take up to 3 days. Do be aware you must have a UK phone number. Any questions – give us a call on 020 7703 7393 and we will try to help.
