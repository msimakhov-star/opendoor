You do not need proof of address or immigration status, ID or an NHS number. Register Online Before completing our online registration form please check that you live within our practice area (see below). We do not accept any registrations from outside of this area. Register at the Surgery If you prefer to complete a paper form, or print out a PDF registration form, our reception team can help you register with us. Please come in between 8:30am and 6pm and bring: The name and address of your previous NHS GP, or the date you have entered the country if you don't have a previous GP. Details of your current and previous address. When registering infants please bring their red book if you have one. If you do not speak English or find it hard to complete forms for any reason please let us know so that we can help with the paperwork. Please ask our reception team if you would like information about registering as a temporary patient.
