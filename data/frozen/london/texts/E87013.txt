Any person living in England or Wales can register with us but only those living within our designated local catchment area can receive our full range of services. Home visits and some locally commissioned community services are not available to patients from outside our catchment area. You can only be registered with one NHS GP practice at a time but for emergency situations temporary registration elsewhere is allowed for a period of up to two weeks.
