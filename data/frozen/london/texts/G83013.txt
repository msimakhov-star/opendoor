This information may be added to your record and will allow us to begin to provide you with medical services. Please complete one form for each family member. Once completed, you will receive text message notification to confirm registration with us. We are happy to register new NHS patients within our practice boundary area. You must live within our practice boundary to register with us. We do not need proof of ID and proof of address to register with the surgery as a patient. If you would like to have online access to your record, we do require photo ID. We will issue linkage key via email within 10 working days for you to register on NHS App and access online services or record. Alternatively, you can register for the NHS App directly and complete online self-verification to access online services without having to contact us.
