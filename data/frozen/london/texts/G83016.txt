Registering with the surgery To register with the surgery: use the NHS register with a GP surgery: Royal Arsenal Medical Centre service or, please visit the practice When you register, it’s helpful to have your NHS number. You can use the NHS website to find your NHS number. Boundary area Anyone looking to register with our practice must have an address within the below postcodes: SE18 6** SE18 7** SE18 9** SE28 0** If you’ve previously registered with a GP surgery, you’ll need your NHS number to register with us, allowing us to transfer your health records electronically. You can find your NHS number on the NHS website. Alternatively, you may visit the surgery to fill out a paper version of the registration form. If you would like access to your online medical records, we ask that you visit the surgery and provide two forms of ID: one for proof of identity and one for proof of address.
