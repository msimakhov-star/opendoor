This can now be done by completing an online form, see below. Please be aware we are a training practice, see more information regarding this here Catchment Area Registrations for patients residing outside the practice catchment area If clinically appropriate and practical, we accept new registrations from patients working in the local area but residing outside of our practice catchment area. Patients registered this way would not be entitled to a home visit and some other community services. For further information about this type of registration, please contact us on 0203 005 2309. How to Register Please follow the link below to complete the online registration form. Adult New Patient Registration Form Under 16’s New Patient Registration Form Please bring a photo ID and proof of address when you next visit the practice. If you do not have this, you can still register with us, please speak to reception. Registration normally takes 3 working days. If you require urgent medical attention during this period, please attend your local urgent care centre or call 111. Before registering, please ensure you have enough medication prescribed from your previous GP for at least 28 days.
