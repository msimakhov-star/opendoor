Centre 020 8803 6705 Skip to content Home Services Team Patient info Care and support About Patient group News Search for: Home Patient information How to register We have an open list and welcome requests for registration from patients living in the N18 postcode or moving into the practice area. If you live in the N18 postcode and wish to register a new born baby please attend the surgery for further advice. Please do not use the online form. You can register by either: COMPLETING THE ONLINE REGISTRATION FORM https://reg.ht1.uk/register-with-gp/F85666 or scan the QR code You will have a named, accountable doctor who is responsible for coordinating your care. You can still talk to or make appointments to see any of our doctors or nurses, not just your named GP. We recommend that new patients undertake a health check with a practice nurse. Your registration will usually be responded to within 2 working days. Temporary patients If you are not registered with the surgery you will be unable to see one of our doctors unless you have a serious emergency. In most other cases, calling 111 is a better option. If you are not registered but need treatment at the surgery you will need to complete a temporary registration form. You can be registered as a temporary patient for up to three months. This means you can be registered with our practice and remain a patient of your permanent GP. After three months you will have to re-register as a temporary patient or permanently register with our practice.
