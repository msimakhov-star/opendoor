This information may be added to your record and will allow us to begin to provide you with medical services. If you had registered with a GP surgery before, you will need your NHS number to register with us so we can get your health records transferred electronically. You can find your NHS number here. Please complete one form for each family member. Once completed, you will receive text message notification to confirm registration with us. We are happy to register new NHS patients within our practice boundary area. You must live within our practice boundary to register with us. We do not need proof of ID or written proof of address for you to register with the surgery as a patient. But, if you would like to have online access to your record, in order to provide this, we do require photo ID and proof of address. Alternatively, you can register for the NHS App and complete online self-verification directly. Please contact the surgery on 0333 332 7300 for further information.
