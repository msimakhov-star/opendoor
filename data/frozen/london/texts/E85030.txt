As it often takes some time for records to be forwarded from your former practice all newly registered patients will be asked to complete a health questionnaire and are offered a consultation with the doctor. Practice Area If you would like to check whether you are resident within our Practice area and so eligible to register with us a Practice then please enter your postcode below. Please note we generally only take patients with a W4 postcode, however if you are in a W6 postcode and pay your council tax to Hounslow we are happy to register you and family. Catchment Area Overseas Visitors if you are visiting or moving to England from abroad and want more information about NHS services please see the NHS England Website for information – Further information for overseas visitors Temporary Registration If you’re currently staying within our GP surgery area (W4 postcode) for more than 24 hours but less than 3 months and have a family member or friend registered with us, you can ask to register as a temporary resident. You can find more information on registering as a temporary resident on the NHS website. Registering with a GP as a temporary resident – NHS – NHS (www.nhs.uk) If you want to register as a temporary resident at our GP surgery, please contact us and we will send you the relevant registration form.
