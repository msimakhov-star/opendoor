RELATED Temporary Registration Non-English Speakers Update Your Details CONTACT US 020 8390 5321 Mon–Fri: 8:00am – 6:30pm Emergencies: call 999 Non-urgent: call 111 Register as a New Patient If you live within our catchment area of Surbiton and nearby areas, you are welcome to register with Brunswick Surgery. Registration is free and can be completed online in around 10 minutes. Are You Eligible to Register? To register with Brunswick Surgery you must live within our catchment area. We have recently extended our boundary to cover a wider area — use the tool below to check whether your postcode is within our catchment. Enter your postcode in the search tool below to instantly check whether your address falls within our catchment area. If you have any difficulty using the tool, call us on 020 8390 5321 and we will be happy to check for you. Anyone can register with an NHS GP — you do not need proof of address, immigration status, or identification to register. However, providing these details helps us to register you as quickly as possible. Who Can Register? Adults and children of any age UK residents and overseas visitors Patients without a fixed address Asylum seekers and refugees If you are registering a child, a parent or guardian must complete the registration form on their behalf. Register Online The quickest and easiest way to register is using our online registration form. The form takes around 10 minutes to complete, and we will aim to get you registered within 2 working days. Before you start, it is helpful to have the following to hand: Your NHS number (if you know it — not essential) Your date of birth Your current address and postcode Your previous GP's name and address (if applicable) Details of any regular medications Register Online → If at any point you find the online form difficult to complete, you are welcome to visit the surgery in person and a member of our team will be happy to help you register another way. Prefer to register in person? Visit us at Surbiton Health Centre, Ewell Road, Surbiton, KT6 6EZ, Monday to Friday between 8:00am and 6:30pm. No appointment needed to register. What Happens After You Register? Once we receive your registration form we will: Process your registration within 2 working days Contact your previous GP practice to request your medical records Send you confirmation once your registration is complete While your registration is being processed you can still contact the surgery if you have an urgent medical need — please call us on 020 8390 5321. New Patient Health Check Once you are registered, you may be invited for a new patient health check with one of our healthcare assistants. This is a routine check that helps us understand your health needs and ensure you receive the most appropriate care. Already a Patient? If you are already registered with us and need to update your contact details, address or other information, please visit our Update Your Details page. Other Registration Options Temporary Registration Visiting the area and need to see a GP? You can register as a temporary patient without leaving your current practice. Temporary Registration → Non-English Speakers Information and support for patients who do not speak English as a first language, including interpreter services. Non-English Speakers → Online Services Once registered, set up your NHS App to manage appointments, prescriptions and results online. Online Services → Contact Us Have a question about registering? Call us or visit the surgery and our team will be happy to help.
