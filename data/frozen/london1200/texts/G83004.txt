For students, we will accept a letter from a landlord or college. You can provide this documentation either by emailing it to barnardmedicalgroup@nhs.net or submitting it in person to one of our receptionists at either site, who will make a copy. Please ensure this information is provided within 14 days of completing your online registration. Please contact the surgery 48 hours after you have submitted your online form to schedule a new patient screening with our Healthcare Assistant. This brief health check includes a blood pressure assessment and a urine test and is available to all new patients. If you are currently on regular medication, you will also need to schedule an appointment with a doctor. If any issues arise following your online registration, a member of the practice team will contact you directly. Please note that by submitting the online form, you will be transmitting personal information over the internet. While every effort is made to secure this information, absolute security cannot be guaranteed.
