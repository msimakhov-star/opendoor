Safe Surgeries We are a Safe Surgery for everyone in our practice area: Everyone living in England has the right to free care from a GP. We might ask for ID or proof of address. But if you don’t have any and you live in our practice area, you can still register with us. If you are worried about giving us your address, please let us know. Your information is safe with us. We won’t ask for immigration documents and our receptionists won’t ask you about your immigration status. Ask reception for an interpreter if you find it difficult to communicate in English. Practice Area Before registering with the practice, please enter your postcode below to make sure you live within our practice area. All local patients are welcome to register for GP services but we are unable to provide home visits to those who live outside of the E11 post code. Please allow up to two weeks for your registration to be confirmed. ID is not mandatory but may be requested to confirm your details. Patients not living in the E10 or E11 postal areas may be asked to register elsewhere at the discretion of the practice.
