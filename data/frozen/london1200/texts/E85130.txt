Non-urgent advice: STEP 1: Check if you live in our catchment area to avoid disappointment We can only register patients who currently live within the Borough of Ealing (postcode W3/W4/W5) and within the boundaries as shown on the map below. If your address is located outside of our catchment area, you will not be able to complete the New Patient Registration form. Check what documents you may be asked to provide: New Patient Registration Documents Non-urgent advice: STEP 4: Confirmation of registration We aim to register you as quickly as possible, typically within 10 working days. We will send you an email confirming you have been registered. If you have not heard from us after 10 working days, please email us citing the reference number allocated to your New Patient Registration form.
