You can register with the practice online. Step 1: Check you live in our catchment area We can only register patients who currently live within our catchment area. Please enter your postcode below to check if your address is within our area. Catchment Area Step 2: Complete Online Registration Form, using link below: Online New Patient Registration Form – Church End Medical Centre OR Complete Paper Registration Form, download (link below) and complete PRF1- New Patient Registration Paper Form Step 3: Email nhsnwl.cemc@nhs.net with two forms of Acceptable ID Two forms of Acceptable ID (1 Photo ID & 1 Proof of address) – if this is not possible, please let us know and see this Safe Surgeries Leaflet. We will not store these documents and we will securely delete/destroy them after our initial verification. Acceptable Photo Identification: Photo Driving Licence, Passport or European ID Card. Acceptable Proof of Address: Tenancy agreement, Mortgage statement, Bank statement, Utility bill (date within the past 3 months) etc. For children under 16 years of age, parents/ guardians should aim to provide a copy of Birth certificate and Immunisation record (in English). Step 4: Optional: Download both the NHS App & PATCHS Use the NHS App first to create your NHS Login (it may require you to upload a copy of your Photo ID). It can help if you have your NHS number to hand. You can use the same NHS Login to access our online appointment booking system PATCHS Visit our Online Services webpage for more guidance. Both these apps will enhance your digital experience as a patient at this practice. Step 5: Await confirmation that you have been registered We aim to register you as quickly as possible. If you have given us a UK mobile phone number, we will send you a text message to confirm you have been registered. If you do not receive such a message, you can email us at nhsnwl.cemc@nhs.net for an update.
