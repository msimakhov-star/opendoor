Whether you’re a resident, student, or professional working in the city, registering with a local NHS GP surgery ensures quick access to healthcare when you need it most. Registering is quick and easy—you don’t need proof of ID, address, or an NHS number. You can register online in minutes or visit us in person at our GP practice in St Johns Wood. Who can register? Anyone can register with a GP in London, whether you live in the area or commute for work or study. Many patients prefer to register with a GP near work or university rather than their home address. To check if you live inside our catchment area, enter your postcode below. If you live outside our practice boundary, you may still be eligible to register as an out-of-area patient. Safe Surgeries – Your Right to Register with an NHS GP Everyone in the UK has the right to free care from a GP. As part of the Safe Surgeries initiative: ✔ You do not need proof of ID or address to register. ✔ Your immigration status does not affect your right to register. ✔ Our receptionists will never ask about your immigration status. ✔ Your details are confidential and will not be shared with the Home Office unless serious crime is involved. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) about GP Registration 1. Can I register with a GP without proof of address? Yes, you do not need proof of address to register. 2. How long does GP registration take? Online applications take 2-3 working days to process. 3. Can I change my GP surgery? Yes, you can switch GP surgeries at any time. Simply register with a new practice, and your records will be transferred automatically. 4. What if I need urgent care before my registration is complete? If you need urgent care, call 111 for non-emergencies or 999 for emergencies. 5. Can students register with your GP practice? Yes! If you are a university student in London, you can register with us—even if your home address is outside London. Register with our GP in London Today Looking for an NHS GP near you?
