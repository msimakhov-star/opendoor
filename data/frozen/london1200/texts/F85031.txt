Are you in our area? To register with Westbury, you’ll need to live within our practice catchment area. You can check by viewing the map below, or simply call reception on 020 8888 3021 and we’ll be happy to confirm. Catchment Area If you are already a patient and move out of the area, you will need to register with a practice closer to your new home. If you change your name, address, or phone number, please let us know so we can keep your records up to date. Read more about our catchment area → What to bring You do not need proof of address or proof of ID to register, though bringing them can help us sort out any queries more quickly. Registering a new baby? Please bring the Red Book and the hospital discharge paperwork. A birth certificate is not required, but baby will need a name. Your named GP Every patient at Westbury is allocated a named GP who has overall responsibility for your care. You are always free to see any of our doctors — your named GP is there for continuity, not to limit your choice. If you are unsure who your named GP is, just ask at reception. Our practice policies Like all GP practices, we have policies on safety in the building and on prescribing for chronic pain. These help us provide consistent, safe care for everyone.
