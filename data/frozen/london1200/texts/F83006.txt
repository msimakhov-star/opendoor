Outside our catchment area Even if you live outside our catchment area, you’re welcome to register under the ‘Out of Area’ scheme — note that home visits and some other services won’t be available to you. Register first and if you are out of area, our admin team will be in touch. You’re here temporarily Staying for a while? ONCE YOU’RE REGISTERED Do more with the NHS App Owned and run by the NHS, the app is a simple, secure way to book appointments, order repeat prescriptions and view your record — from your phone. Learn more & download Good to know Visiting or staying temporarily? If you’re ill away from home or not registered with a GP, you can get emergency treatment from a local practice for up to 14 days. After that, register as a temporary or permanent patient. You can be a temporary patient for up to three months while staying registered with your usual GP. Contact the practice you’d like to use — you can’t register temporarily where you’re already registered. More about temporary registration Your named GP Every patient has a named GP with overall responsibility for their care. We assign patients by surname to share workload fairly, but you can always see any GP in the practice. Prefer a specific doctor? Let reception know and we’ll do our best. Visitors from overseas Visitors from overseas without access to NHS treatment may be seen at the doctor’s discretion, subject to a consultation fee and payment for medication or investigations.
