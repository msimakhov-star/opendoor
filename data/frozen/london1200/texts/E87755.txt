How to Register To register with our practice please follow the links below to complete the registration forms. After you have completed and submitted the form you must email within 7 days 2 forms of identification to queenspark.healthcentre@nhs.net. One of these must contain your address (e.g. tenancy agreement, mortgage agreement, bank statement), and the other to have your photograph (eg. driving licence, passport, for children a birth certificate is adequate). Warning advice: Until you have submitted your ID you will not be registered at the practice and cannot be seen for any treatment.
