How to register You can now register as a new patient using our new online portal by clicking here. Alternatively if you would rather register using a paper form you can follow the steps below: To complete your registration we require the following: NHS Card New Patient Registration Pack Proof of Address (dated within the last 3 months) Proof of Identity NHS Card Your NHS card contains your NHS number and details of your previous GP. If you cannot find your NHS card you can find out your NHS number by contacting your previous GP surgery. New Patient Registration Pack The New Patient Registration pack can be obtained from our Reception. The pack includes: Health Questionnaire Alcohol Questionnaire Ethnicity Form Contact Authorisation Form Patient Access Application Form Proof of Address Proof of address documentation examples (must be dated within the last 3 months): Utility bill (gas, electricity, community charge etc) Phone bill stating address Credit card/bank statement Rent book or tenancy agreement Pension book Benefit/family credit book Home office permit to stay Document showing University/college hall of residence TV license Driving license (with address) HM Revenue and Customs Statements Council Tax Bill/Council Rent book Home Insurance Policy Documentation from a reputable source, for example a letter from a voluntary organisation or a refuge. Your address must be within our designated Practice Area. Proof of Identity This can be one of the following: Passport ID Card Photo Driver’s License (UK only) For all children please also provide the Red Book. This contains important information about your child’s immunisation history and developmental records.
