You can register at reception by using a registration form and supplying us with proof of identity and address. Any patients registering who have come from outside of the UK must provide proof of entry, proof of a visa or home office letter. All new patients will be asked to attend a new patient health check with the practice nurse or a healthcare assistant. This will be arranged when registering. Online ‘Pre-Registration’ With The Practice If you wish to pre-register click on the link below to open the form. When you have completed all of the details, click on the “Send” button to mail your form to us. When you visit the surgery for the first time you will be asked to sign the form to confirm that the details are correct and complete a data sharing consent form. You will also need to bring in two forms of ID, preferably one with a photo. Application to Register with a GP Note that by sending the form you will be transmitting information about your self across the Internet and although every effort is made to keep this information secure, no guarantee can be offered in this respect.
