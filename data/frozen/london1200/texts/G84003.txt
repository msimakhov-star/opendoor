We can only accept patients who live within our boundary area. To check if you fall within our catchment, please use the postcode checker below. Catchment Area How to Register To register with our practice please click the following links: https://register-with-gp.ht1.uk?gpCode=G84003&gpName=Links%20Medical%20Practice – if your home address fall under our Downham branch https://register-with-gp.ht1.uk?gpCode=G84003001&gpName=Links%20Medical%20Practice – if your home address falls under our Mottingham branch or alternatively, you can register with the practice via the NHS App. If you are unable to register via the above methods please come into the surgery and collect a registration form to complete or you can print the GMS1 and registration form from our website and once completed bring into the practice. Please bring two forms of identification and proof of address not older than three months (eg bank statement, utility bill) to reception with your completed GMS1 and registration form. Acceptable identification includes: Passport Birth Certificate HC2 Certificate Rough sleepers’ identity badge Hostel Registration/mail forwarding letter. Proof of address not older than three months (eg bank statement, utility bill) For further information on registering with your GP, please visit the NHS website.
