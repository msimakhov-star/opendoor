Contact Us Register Now REGISTER WITH OUR NHS GP PRACTICE IN STOCKWELL No ID, no proof of address, no NHS number needed — we are an open NHS practice accepting new patients from anywhere in London, and registration takes about 10 minutes online. https://vimeo.com/728911454 Join Our Practice Binfield Road Surgery is a friendly, patient-centred NHS GP in Stockwell, currently accepting new patient registrations from anywhere in London. You do not need to live within our catchment area to register — we are an open practice and welcome all patients. You do not need proof of address or ID to register. Everyone is entitled to NHS care. How to Register Step 1: Complete the Online Registration Form Fill in and submit the registration form. This takes about 10 minutes and gives us your main details. You do not need to visit the surgery. Registration Form Or register and book in one step Use our Bookable service to register as a new patient and book your first appointment at the same time. Register & Book Online Step 2: Download the NHS App Register with the NHS App to gain access to all our online services — book appointments, order repeat prescriptions, view your test results, and message the practice securely. It is quick and free to set up. NHS app Step 3: Book a New Patient Health Check If you are aged 40–74 or have a long-term condition, we recommend booking a health check once you are registered. This is a free NHS service. Opening hours: Monday to Friday, 8:00am – 6:30pm. Common Questions Do I need ID or proof of address? No. You do not strictly need proof of address or ID to register with a GP. However, if you have them, uploading helps us match your NHS record faster. Can I register if I live outside your catchment area? Yes. We are an open practice and accept registrations from anywhere in London. The only limitation is that home visits are only available within our catchment area. All other services are fully available to out-of-area patients. I have a child or baby to register? You can register children using the same online form. Have their NHS number handy (found in the Red Book) if possible. Asylum seekers and refugees You do not need a fixed address, immigration documents, or NHS number to register. Everyone is entitled to NHS care. How long does registration take to process? You will usually receive confirmation of your registration by email.
