The website will ask for you postcode, once you have entered this it will give you a list of GP surgeries close to your address, from which you can select “Gables Surgery” (Markyate Road, Dagenham, Essex RM8 2LD). You can register in person by coming to the surgery reception and completing a new patient registration form. You can also click below to download a copy of the New Patient Registration Form to complete and bring with you or complete the online registration form below. Online Registration Form Registration Form (PDF) When you come to register, please bring with you two forms of identification. One photo ID, for example a driving license or passport, and also a document that can prove your current address, for example a utility or council tax bill, or a rent/ bank statement. We will also accept birth certificates, or a letter from an official sponsor (such as Doctor of the World) as proof of ID if you do not have photo ID. We advise when registering in person that you come to the surgery after 12pm, when the reception is less busy. We look forward to seeing you!
