from the selection. You will be asked to fill a form and submit your personal information so we can register you. All new registration applications are usually processed within 5 working days, however on some occasions it can take up a little longer depending on the volume of applications received. Am I registering with the right practice? Please note that you must live within the practice boundary to be able to register with us. If you’re unsure, please view our Location Map. Everyone in the UK has the right to free care from a GP. If you don’t have proof of ID or address, you can still register. We aim to include all individual, meaning your immigration status or nationality doesn’t matter.If you need any support or have any queries regarding registration please do not hesitate to speak to a member of our team.
